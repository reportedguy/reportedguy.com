Product Management Intern, Huminex India Pvt Ltd · Jan to Mar 2026 · Delhi

A live store, first analytics and consistent catalogues for a wholesale brand

I owned the digital expansion of Huminex, a B2B wholesale brand, and worked directly with the founder.

The situation

Huminex sold wholesale, mostly offline. There was no store, no order tracking and no analytics. Catalogues looked different from one category to the next. Each of these was a gap I could close.

What I did

  • Built and launched the e-commerce store on WordPress and WooCommerce in three weeks, with Razorpay and PayPal Business payments. I set up the domain and hosting on Hostinger.
  • Built a custom order management dashboard with the founder: live product listing, inventory and payment tracking, where there had been no tooling at all.
  • Set up Google Analytics, the business's first traffic and conversion data.
  • Created a design system and catalogue templates, and redesigned catalogues across 15+ categories and 250+ SKUs.
  • Built a product photography engine on n8n. I led the team that produced the photography and applied the design system to new categories.

What came of it

A live store, the business's first traffic data, and catalogues that look consistent across categories. That improved how the brand comes across in offline sales and client presentations.

I ran communication with the founder throughout.
