Product Intern, Teen.fit × Cult.fit · Jul to Sep 2025 · Remote

A teen wellness app, taken from research to a pitched MVP

I took Teen.fit, a wellness app for ages 14 to 20, from zero to a pitched MVP, starting with research on 200+ teens.

The situation

Teen.fit was a new wellness product for teenagers, run with Cult.fit. When I joined there was no scope, no design and no build. The first job was to find out what teens actually needed before deciding what to make.

What I did

  1. Led market research with 200+ teens: an Adolescent Health and Wellness Survey plus a competitor analysis.
  2. Wrote the PRD. I defined the MVP scope, chose the tech stack, ranked features and set the build timeline.
  3. Built the design identity and a brand mascot, so the product felt the same on every channel.
  4. Designed user flows, wireframes and 20+ high-fidelity screens in Figma.
  5. Built a video-based MVP and partly implemented screens in FlutterFlow.

What came of it

I pitched the product vision and MVP to senior management consultants at EY, at the Shangri-La in New Delhi.

The research came first: a survey and a competitor analysis, written up as a PRD before anything was built.
