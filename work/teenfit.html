Teen.fit: research first, then the product

I took a teen wellness app for ages 14 to 20 from zero to a pitched MVP, starting with research on 200+ teens.

Context

Teen.fit was a new wellness product for teenagers, run with Cult.fit. There was no scope, no design and no build when I joined. The first job was to find out what teens actually needed before deciding what to make.

What I did

  • Led market research with 200+ teens, combining an Adolescent Health and Wellness Survey with a competitor analysis.
  • Wrote the PRD, defined the MVP scope, chose the tech stack, ranked features and set the build timeline.
  • Built the design identity and a brand mascot so the product felt the same on every channel.
  • Designed user flows, wireframes and 20+ high-fidelity screens in Figma.
  • Built a video-based MVP and partly implemented screens in FlutterFlow.

Result

I pitched the product vision and MVP to senior management consultants at EY, at the Shangri-La in New Delhi.

Artifacts

  • FigJam board research, planning and flows
  • Figma file UI and high-fidelity screens
  • MVP video the video-based MVP
