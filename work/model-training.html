A small sorting model that scored 92.9% on Banking77, on a laptop CPU

A tool that turns a spreadsheet of labelled text into a small sorting model that runs on your own computer, says when it is unsure, and improves as you correct it.

The situation

Many enterprise AI jobs are narrow: route a support ticket, tag a request. A small model trained for that one task can be cheap, private and fast.

What came of it

On the public Banking77 dataset (customer-service questions, 77 categories), a model built through the tool scored 92.9% on the 3,080-question test set. It ran on a laptop CPU with no GPU, at about 7 milliseconds per request.
