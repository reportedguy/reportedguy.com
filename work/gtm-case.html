BeyondTech Case Competition 2026 · hosted by DCG with BeyondTech · team Think Tank

A go-to-market plan for BeyondTech. It won.

BeyondTech trains students for non-tech roles. My team, Think Tank, built its go-to-market strategy from primary and secondary research, and won the competition and its ₹10,000 cash prize.

The problem

BeyondTech already did well at DTU, but most of its reach ran through one founder's LinkedIn, and outside a few campuses few students knew the brand. The question: how does it grow at DTU and launch at three or four Tier-1 campuses with a plan it can afford and measure?

What we did

  • Primary research: a student survey, plus a map of when students actually decide to buy, from second year to placement season.
  • Secondary research: the company's own results, public student discussion, and its competitors.
  • Five buyer personas, each tied to a cohort start date.
  • A weighted scorecard to rank nine campuses, picking four new ones in two waves, with a go or no-go check after week four.
  • A budget, unit economics and stress tests that show where the plan breaks even.

What I took from it

A plan is only as good as the numbers behind it, so every figure in the deck carries a tag for where it came from. An estimate never passes for a fact.
