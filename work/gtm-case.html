A go-to-market plan for BeyondTech that won the case competition

My team, Think Tank, built BeyondTech's go-to-market strategy from primary and secondary research, and won the competition and its ₹10,000 cash prize.

The situation

BeyondTech trains students for non-tech roles. It already did well at DTU, but most of its reach ran through one founder's LinkedIn. Outside a few campuses, few students knew the brand.

The question: how does it grow at DTU and launch at three or four Tier-1 campuses, with a plan it can afford and measure?

What we did

  • Primary research: a student survey, plus a map of when students actually decide to buy, from second year to placement season.
  • Secondary research: the company's own results, public student discussion, and its competitors.
  • Five buyer personas, each tied to a cohort start date.
  • A weighted scorecard to rank nine campuses. It picked four new ones in two waves, with a go or no-go check after week four.
  • A budget, unit economics and stress tests that show where the plan breaks even.

How the numbers were handled
